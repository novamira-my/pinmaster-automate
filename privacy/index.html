2. What the Service does

PinMaster Automate lets you schedule and publish content to Pinterest business accounts that you own. You connect your own Pinterest accounts through Pinterest's official authorisation flow, upload your own images, supply your own titles and descriptions, and choose when each pin should publish.

It is a delivery tool. It does not write, rewrite, or alter your content, and it has no engagement features.

4. What we do not do

  • We never ask for your Pinterest password. Authorisation is always through Pinterest's own OAuth flow. If anything ever asks for your Pinterest password on our behalf, it is not us.
  • We do not access Pinterest accounts you have not connected.
  • We do not collect data about other Pinterest users. No follower lists, no other people's profiles, no scraping of public Pinterest content.
  • We do not combine your data with another user's. Each account's data is isolated — see section 7.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.
  • We do not use your content or your Pinterest data to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not use unofficial access methods. The Service uses only the official Pinterest API v5. No browser automation, no undocumented endpoints, no techniques to evade platform controls or rate limits.
  • We do not perform engagement automation. No following, commenting, liking, repinning, or messaging.

7. How your data is protected

  • Isolation between accounts. Every record belongs to exactly one account, and every query is filtered by account. This is enforced in the data access layer rather than left to individual queries, and it is covered by tests that attempt cross-account access and expect it to fail.
  • Token encryption. Pinterest access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM envelope encryption. The master key is held in a managed key-management service, never in source code or plain environment variables.
  • Password storage. Passwords are stored only as a hash produced by a function designed for password hashing. We cannot read your password, and we will never email it to you.
  • Private media storage. Uploaded images are stored in a private object-storage bucket, partitioned per account. There is no public bucket and no CDN. Objects are never publicly readable; your browser reaches them only through short-lived signed URLs issued to your own session.
  • Images are never exposed at a public URL. Pins are created using the Pinterest API's image_base64 source type, so image data travels from private storage into a single API request. It is never placed at a fetchable public address.
  • Transport security. All traffic is encrypted with TLS.
  • Least privilege. We request only the Pinterest permissions we actively use — see section 8.

10. Keeping, deleting, and disconnecting

Connected account identifiersDeleted when you disconnect that account
Uploaded images and pin recordsUntil you delete them, or until you delete your account

Technical logsRetained for 30 days, then deleted

Disconnecting a Pinterest account. You can disconnect any connected account from within the Service at any time, which deletes its stored tokens immediately. You can also revoke our access directly from Pinterest, under Settings, then Security and permissions, then Apps. We detect a revoked token, stop making requests for that account, and mark it as needing reconnection.

Deleting your account. You can delete your PinMaster Automate account from your account settings. This is a real deletion, not a flag: your uploaded images are removed from storage, your pin records and account details are deleted, and every Pinterest token is destroyed. Pins already published to Pinterest remain on Pinterest — they are yours, and only you can remove them there.

You can also request deletion by writing to clicknquest@gmail.com, and we will complete it within 30 days.
