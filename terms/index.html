5. Availability, scheduling, and accuracy

The App is provided without any uptime or delivery guarantee.

This clause matters more than it usually would, so it is worth stating plainly: the Pinterest API does not support scheduled publication. A pin becomes public at the moment the App successfully creates it. The App therefore holds each pin until its scheduled time and sends it then.

The practical consequence: if the App is unavailable when a pin is due — because of a server failure, a network outage, a Pinterest API error, maintenance, or anything else — that pin may be published late, or not published at all. The App applies a configurable grace period and marks anything outside it as failed rather than publishing it at an unintended time.

Nothing in the App should be relied upon for time-critical publication.
